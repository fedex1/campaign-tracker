(January 12, 2022 to January 11, 2026)

Participants in the Campaign Finance Program may not accept contributions from political committees unless the committees are registered with the Campaign Finance Board. The following is the current list of registered political committees, which includes all candidate committees for candidates who have filed with the CFB. Candidates should make sure a political committee is registered with the Campaign Finance Board before depositing a contribution.

All candidates are prohibited from accepting contributions, directly or indirectly, from corporations, limited liability companies (LLCs), limited liability partnerships (LLPs), and partnerships. This ban does not apply to contributions from political committees that are themselves incorporated or LLCs, LLPs, or partnerships.

Candidates must demonstrate that they are not accepting indirect prohibited contributions from political committees. To assist candidates in determining whether or not the contributions they are receiving from political committees are from prohibited sources, the Campaign Finance Board now asks registered political committees if they accepted contributions from corporations, LLCs, LLPs, and partnerships, and if they agree not to use those funds for contributions to candidates. Candidates should contact the political committee directly if they have a question regarding contributions from prohibited sources.

To locate a particular committee or candidate, use the search function in your browser (CTRL+F).
